Build a web-based UK Second-hand Motor Dealer Stock Book app for accountants to provide to motor trade clients using the VAT Margin Scheme.

Use:
- Next.js or React frontend
- Node/Express backend if needed
- SQLite or PostgreSQL database
- Clean, simple, mobile-friendly design
- UK date format: DD/MM/YYYY
- Currency in GBP
- British English throughout

Purpose:
The app must track every second-hand vehicle individually from purchase to sale and calculate the VAT Margin Scheme figures. It must store the stock book, purchase list and sales list, and allow the user to export each list at a date selected by the user.

HMRC basis:
The stock book must track each margin scheme item individually, including purchase details, sale details, margin and VAT due. VAT due is one-sixth / 16.67% of a positive margin. VAT must not be shown separately on the customer invoice. Records should be exportable and auditable.

Core database fields:

Vehicle / Stock Record:
- id
- stock_number, unique, numerical sequence preferred, autogenerated
- registration_number
- make
- model
- mileage_at_purchase
- purchase_date
- purchase_invoice_number
- purchase_type: private purchase / auction / trade / part exchange / other
- seller_name
- seller_address
- purchase_price
- purchase_notes
- purchase_document_reference
- status: in stock / sold / disposed / returned
- sale_date
- sales_invoice_number
- buyer_name
- buyer_address
- sale_price
- method_of_disposal: retail sale / trade sale / auction / scrapped / returned / other
- sale_notes
- margin = sale_price minus purchase_price
- vat_due = if margin is positive, margin / 6, otherwise 0
- net_margin = margin minus vat_due
- created_at
- updated_at

Important rules:
- Stock number must be unique.
- Purchase price must not include repairs, valeting, warranty costs or overheads.
- A vehicle cannot be marked as sold unless sale date, sales invoice number, buyer name, buyer address and sale price are entered.
- VAT due must only be calculated on a positive margin.
- If sale price is less than purchase price, VAT due is £0.
- Sales invoices must not show VAT separately.
- Add warning text: “Margin Scheme sale: VAT must not be shown separately on the invoice.”
